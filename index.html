Privacy Policy Terms of Service Security & Encryption

Duolign is a mobile application developed in the UK. Open Banking features are brokered by our regulated partner, an FCA-authorised Account Information Services Provider (AISP) — Duolign operates under their regulatory permissions rather than holding its own. Connections are read-only: Duolign can see your balances and transactions, and can never move money or make payments. Duolign uses bank-grade encryption in transit and at rest, and never stores your raw online banking credentials.

Forecasts, debt-payoff dates and what-if projections are estimates based on the information you provide and your recent transaction history. They are not financial advice.
