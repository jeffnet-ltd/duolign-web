Payday

From payday scramble to five minutes.

The maths runs across both incomes and every joint obligation. You get a checklist.

  • A numbered transfer checklist, to the penny
    Exact amounts, in order. Work down the list, tap each amount to copy it into your banking app, done.
  • Your personal allowance is protected
    Once the joint transfers are done, whatever’s left in your personal account is yours to spend. No oversight, no guilt.

Partner approval

No one changes the household plan alone.

Any tweak to a shared target needs a tap from both phones before it takes effect.

  • Request sent
    One partner proposes a change — say, increasing the holiday fund target.
  • Partner reviews
    The other partner sees exactly what’s changing, and why.
  • Plan updates
    Only once both agree does the shared plan actually change.

Your money should be shared by agreement, not changed by surprise.

One view

Every account, card and pot. One place.

Stop opening four banking apps to work out what the household actually has left this month.

  • Upload a bank statement
    A PDF or CSV export from Monzo, Starling, Barclays, Lloyds, HSBC and more — Duolign reads the transactions straight off it, no bank login required. (Open Banking account linking is coming soon.)

Look forward

See where the money’s taking you, not just where it went.

Duolign models the months ahead so a decision made today has a number attached to it.

  • Surplus routed to your goals
    Once the bills and baselines are covered, the household surplus goes to the targets you both set — a deposit, a wedding, next year’s holiday.
  • Avalanche and Snowball, side by side
    Avalanche saves the most interest; Snowball clears balances fastest. Duolign shows the exact month you’re debt-free on either plan.
  • Drag a slider, watch 12 months unfold
    Move the salary slider, add a car payment, pause the debt overpayment — and watch the surplus, personal allowance and debt-free date ripple out over the next 1, 6 or 12 months. Your scenario is remembered on your device so you can pick it back up later — it never touches your real accounts or money.

Pricing Privacy Policy Terms of Service Security & Encryption

Duolign is a mobile application developed in the UK. At launch, Duolign works from a bank statement you upload or figures you enter by hand — no bank login required. Open Banking account linking is coming in a future update, brokered by a regulated partner, an FCA-authorised Account Information Services Provider (AISP), under whose regulatory permissions Duolign will operate rather than holding its own; those connections will be read-only and can never move money or make payments. Duolign uses bank-grade encryption in transit and at rest, and never stores your raw online banking credentials.

Forecasts, debt-payoff dates and what-if projections are estimates based on the information you provide and your recent transaction history. They are not financial advice.
