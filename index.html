Pricing FAQs Privacy Policy Terms of Service Security & Encryption

Duolign is a mobile application developed in the UK. Duolign works from a bank statement you upload or figures you enter by hand — no bank login required, and Duolign can never move money or make payments. Duolign uses bank-grade encryption in transit and at rest, and never asks for your online banking login.

Forecasts, debt-payoff dates and what-if projections are estimates based on the information you provide and your recent transaction history. They are not financial advice.
