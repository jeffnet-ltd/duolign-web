1. What the programme is

The programme lets businesses that work with couples introduce them to Duolign, an app that helps couples run their money together. You receive complimentary Premium vouchers to give to clients, and you can buy further voucher packs. You aren't paid by Duolign, and you don't pay anything to take part.

2. Who can take part

The programme is for UK businesses, including sole traders, whose clients include couples. Duolign decides which businesses to enrol and may decline any application. Only enrolled businesses can sign in to a partner page.

3. Your complimentary vouchers

  • When you're enrolled, you receive three complimentary vouchers.
  • Each voucher gives one couple three months of Duolign Premium, starting when they redeem it.
  • Each voucher can be sent and redeemed for one year from the day it's issued. After that, it expires.
  • Vouchers have no cash value and can't be exchanged, sold or transferred, except by sending them to a client as described below.

4. Sending a voucher to a client

  • You send a voucher by entering your client's email address on your partner page. Duolign then emails the voucher to them, in your business's name.
  • You must have your client's permission before entering their email address. Tell them that you're sending them a Duolign voucher and that Duolign will email them about it.
  • Send each voucher to one client only, using an email address they use themselves.
  • Don't send vouchers to yourself, your staff, or your own business email addresses, and don't send several vouchers to the same client.
  • A voucher can only be redeemed by an account with the email address it was sent to. Until it's redeemed, you can correct the address and resend it.

5. Your first-pack discount

  • Once you've sent all three complimentary vouchers within 90 days of enrolment, you'll receive a personal code for 20% off your first voucher pack. It appears on your partner page and is emailed to you.
  • Your clients don't need to have redeemed their vouchers for you to qualify.
  • The code can be used once, on one pack, and expires 180 days after your enrolment. It can't be exchanged for cash or combined with other offers.
  • After that, standard published prices apply.

Codes from packs appear on your partner page. You can send them to clients in the same way, or hand them out directly.
