1. What we collect

Account & household data, which you give us directly: your email address, a display name, your household's structure (you and your partner), how you split shared costs, your payday pattern, and the bills, pots, goals and debts you set up.

Financial data, from the bank statements you upload and the figures you enter yourself: balances and transaction history for those accounts.

Device & usage data: a push-notification token (so we can notify you), basic device type, and app diagnostics (crash and error logs) so we can fix problems.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or browsing history outside the app.

2. AI-assisted statement reading

If you upload a PDF bank statement from a bank we don't yet read with a dedicated, rule-based parser, its text is read by an AI language model instead, to extract a list of transactions. This is a model we run ourselves, not a third-party AI product — your statement data is never sent to an external AI company such as OpenAI, Google or Anthropic for this.

Before any text reaches the model, we strip out account numbers, sort codes, card numbers, and your name and address automatically — only the transaction lines themselves (dates, descriptions, amounts) are sent. The model runs on cloud servers located entirely within the European Economic Area (EEA); this data never leaves the EEA for this purpose, and is never used to train any model.

The model's output is never trusted on its own: every transaction it returns is checked, deterministically, against the statement's own printed totals before anything is saved. A statement that doesn't reconcile is rejected rather than silently imported wrong.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the service (performance of our contract with you): calculating your household's payday split, tracking bills and debts, running the projections you ask for, and the two-person approval workflow for shared changes.
  • To keep the service secure (our legitimate interest): detecting misuse, abuse, and unauthorised access attempts.
  • To deliver a voucher a business gave you (our legitimate interest): if a partner business sends you a Duolign voucher, it gives us your email address. We use it only to email you the voucher and to know which business it came from.
  • To send you news and offers (your consent): only if you opt in yourself — through the link in a voucher email, the redeem page, or Settings → Notifications in the app — and you can switch it off there at any time. Every such email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To contact beta applicants (your consent): if you join our closed-beta waiting list on this website, we use your email address only to tell you when a place opens up.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to anyone, ever.

4. Who sees it

Your partner sees the data your household shares by design — that's the point of a joint budget. Every account in a household is visible to both of you.

We share data with a small number of infrastructure providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, and only as needed to run the service:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, hosted in the EU (AWS, Ireland).
  • Railway — hosts the servers that run the app, in the EU (Amsterdam, Netherlands).
  • RunPod — our cloud GPU hosting provider; runs the AI model described in section 2, within the EEA only; it never sees who you are, only redacted statement text.
  • Google Firebase — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Resend — delivers email notifications to us when you contact us (in-app feedback, a deletion request, or joining the beta waiting list), containing your email address and what you wrote. Based in the US; no financial data is ever sent through it.
  • RevenueCat — if you buy Premium in the app, records the subscription so both of you get it. It receives your household's ID (a random code, never your name, email or any financial data) and the purchase details from Apple or Google. Based in the US. Apple and Google take the payment themselves, under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details.
  • Sentry — receives crash and error reports from the app and our servers so we can fix problems, hosted in the EU (Germany).

None of these providers may use your data for their own purposes. We do not share data with data brokers, advertisers, or any other third party.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your household's data for as long as your household is active, so the app keeps working correctly, unless you delete your account or household outright. Beta waiting-list emails are kept until the beta ends or you ask us to remove them. An email address a partner business gave us to send you a voucher is deleted once that voucher expires unused (a year after it was issued) or is withdrawn, unless you've opted in to hear from us; if you redeem it, it's simply your account's own address, and goes when your household is deleted.

6. Your rights — including deleting everything yourself

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. For most of these, you don't need to email anyone:

Settings → delete this household schedules your household's entire record — balances, bills, pots, statements, history — for both of you, for permanent deletion 30 days later.
Settings → delete my account schedules your login and your private data the same way; if your partner has joined the household, it stays for them.
Both are fully reversible — cancel from the same screen any time before the 30 days are up. We recommend exporting your data first — the same screen offers a download.

Don't have the app installed, or would rather not reinstall it just to leave? Use our Delete My Data form instead — we'll verify it's really you, then erase your data the same way.

For anything else — a copy of your data, a correction, or a question about how we've used it — email privacy@duolign.co.uk. We'll respond within one month.

8. International transfers

Your data is primarily stored within the EU, including the AI-assisted statement reading described above (section 2), which never leaves the EEA. Google Firebase (push notifications) may process limited device data outside the UK/EEA; where it does, this happens under Google's standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards recognised under UK GDPR. Resend (email notifications, section 4) stores the email address and message it delivers in the United States, under the EU standard contractual clauses as amended by the UK Addendum; Resend is also certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension. RevenueCat (in-app subscriptions, section 4) processes subscription records in the United States, under the EU standard contractual clauses as amended by the UK Addendum.

9. Cookies & this website

This marketing website (duolign.co.uk) does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If you join the beta waiting list, your email address is sent directly to our database provider to be stored; no analytics or third-party tracking scripts run on this page. The Duolign app itself is a native mobile app and does not use browser cookies.
